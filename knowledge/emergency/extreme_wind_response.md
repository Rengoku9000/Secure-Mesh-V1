# Extreme Wind Response

SecureMesh Demo Operational Knowledge. Written for demonstration; not official
agency guidance.

## Before the wind arrives

Secure or remove anything that can become airborne: sheeting, scaffold boards,
fuel drums, unsecured antennas and temporary signage. Debris causes most wind
injuries, and almost all of it was something nobody tied down.

Lower masts and portable antennas rather than trying to hold them. Move vehicles
away from trees, walls and temporary structures.

## During

Shelter in the interior of a substantial building, on the lowest floor, away
from windows. Put as many walls as possible between people and the outside. Stay
out of temporary structures, portable cabins, vehicles and anything with a large
unsupported roof span.

Do not go outside to check on equipment during the peak. Nothing recoverable is
worth the exposure, and wind speed at the ground is highly variable: a lull is
not the end.

## Vehicle operations

High-sided vehicles are at risk of being blown over on exposed roads, bridges
and embankments. Reduce speed, increase the following distance, and expect
sudden gusts at gaps in tree lines and buildings. If the vehicle is being pushed
across the lane, stop somewhere sheltered and wait.

## After

Treat every downed line as live. Approach damaged structures from upwind and
assume roofing material is loose. Check masts and antenna alignment before
relying on any link, because a link that appears up may be running on a
misaligned antenna and will fail under load.

## Reporting

Report sustained wind and gust estimates separately, the damage observed, routes
blocked by fallen material, and the state of any power or communications
infrastructure.
